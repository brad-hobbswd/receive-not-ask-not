You Have Not Because You Ask Not

A thoughtful exploration of prayer, waiting, disappointment, and the courage to keep bringing our honest questions to God.

Inspired by James 4:2, this book brings personal reflection together with real conversations about faith and everyday life. The stories make room for uncertainty and for experiences that do not end with easy answers.
