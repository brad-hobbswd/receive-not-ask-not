About the book

You Have Not Because You Ask Not

What does it mean to ask God when we are waiting, disappointed, carrying too much, or unsure what to pray?

Inspired by James 4:2, this book considers prayer as an honest relationship with God. It explores the reasons people hesitate to ask, what repeated disappointment can do to hope, and how we might continue seeking God without pretending every story resolves neatly.

Personal reflections are paired with real conversations about prayer, sickness, grief, responsibility, relationships, and the need for help. These accounts are included to listen and understand, not to offer a single explanation for every person's experience.
