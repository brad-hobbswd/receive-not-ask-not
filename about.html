About the author

Bradley Hobbs

Bradley Hobbs writes about faith through personal reflection and honest conversation.

In You Have Not Because You Ask Not, Bradley explores what it means to pray when life is difficult, when answers take time, and when we are not sure what to ask for. The book listens to people with different experiences and makes room for questions that do not have simple answers.

His approach is grounded in listening: honoring what people share, reflecting on Scripture, and resisting the urge to force every story into a tidy ending.
