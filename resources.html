Reading resources

Use these references and prompts to reflect on the book's themes at your own pace.

Scripture references

  • James 4:2–3 — asking and examining our motives.
  • Luke 18:1 — continuing to pray and not losing heart.
  • Matthew 11:28 — bringing weariness and burdens to Jesus.
  • Psalm 13 — expressing sorrow and asking, “How long?”
  • Romans 8:26 — seeking God when we do not know what to pray.
  • Galatians 6:2 — bearing one another's burdens.
  • James 5:16 — praying for one another.

Questions for reflection

  • What has made it easier or harder for you to ask for help?
  • How has waiting shaped what you expect when you pray?
  • What support might you need from another person right now?
  • What would an honest prayer sound like today, even if it is only a few words?
